## Purpose

This matrix completes the source-allocation test in AOS to-do item 4 by accounting for every item in the three-document quality framework: Foundational Principles v4, AOS Reasoning Principles v6, and Governance Intelligence Tests. It records only allocations supported by the current controlled architecture. Missing links are recorded explicitly rather than inferred.

## Source authority

- `Foundational_Principles_v4.docx` — foundational settlement; SHA-256 recorded in the preserved v0.1 source manifest.
- `AOS_Reasoning_Principles_v6.docx` — current supplied reasoning baseline and governing reasoning constraints; individual principles have no official source IDs, so this matrix identifies them by domain and opening phrase.
- `Governance_Intelligence_Tests.docx` — governance assessment framework, M1.1–M1.14 and M2.1–M2.15; these are not solely software tests.
- The preserved source manifest is not silently refreshed. A changed source baseline requires a new manifest/version decision.

## Status meanings

- **Confirmed** — the current AOS-00 baseline explicitly supplies an owning specification/component allocation, requirement link and planned verification link for the source proposition.
- **Partial** — the baseline explicitly applies or allocates the source proposition, but at least one link in specification → requirement → verification remains incomplete or broader than the individual source item.
- **Unresolved** — the current controlled baseline does not yet provide a defensible complete allocation. The row is an explicit traceability finding, not permission to infer one.

**Important:** REQ and VER references below are AOS-00 proposed requirements/planned verification cases, not approved implementation requirements or executed tests.

## 4. Traceability findings

### F-TR-001 — Individual RP traceability is incomplete

RP v6 contains 41 individual principles across nine domains. AOS-00 v0.6 allocates all nine domains at a high level, but many individual principles do not yet have their own requirement and planned verification chain. The unresolved and partial rows above are the explicit findings required by item 4; they must be closed through the responsible component specifications rather than by adding speculative AOS-00 requirements.

### F-TR-002 — Governance Intelligence Tests require AOS-40 catalogue allocation

AOS-00 explicitly identifies only a subset of GT tests as informing current architecture evidence and planned cases. GT M1.13 has a direct REQ/VER chain through AOS-00-REQ-019 / VER-010. The remaining partial/unresolved tests require explicit allocation in AOS-40 and relevant component specifications. Mode 2 tests must retain human/longitudinal assessment where the source requires it; they must not be converted into software-only pass/fail tests.

### F-TR-003 — Foundational Principles are system constraints, not necessarily one-to-one software requirements

All ten FP principles apply to the whole-system architecture, but a principle may legitimately map to several component requirements and human governance instruments rather than one software requirement. FP7 (No-exit obligation) and FP9 (Compliance as consequence) currently lack a sufficiently explicit REQ/VER chain and remain unresolved here.

### F-TR-004 — Source baseline is internally consistent

The three source documents reviewed are the versions named and fingerprinted by the preserved AOS-00 Source Manifest v0.1. No source-version conflict was identified in this traceability exercise. A later source change requires a deliberate new source-manifest version rather than alteration of the preserved v0.1 manifest.

## 5. Disposition

**Item 4 completion criterion:** satisfied at traceability-accounting level. Every FP principle, individual RP principle and GT test now has either a current allocation status or an explicit unresolved finding.

This does **not** mean traceability is complete for build. The 35 unresolved and 28 partial rows become controlled drafting obligations for the relevant component specifications and AOS-40. They should be progressively converted to Confirmed as component REQ/CAP/FUN and conformance cases are approved.

**Next controlled action:** carry the Partial/Unresolved rows into component drafting and AOS-40; do not create architecture-wide requirements merely to make the matrix appear complete.
