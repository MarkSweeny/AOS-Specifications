1. Purpose and standing

An architectural contract defines what an exchange means, who may produce and consume it, what authority it requires, and what each participant must do with its result. An API may implement the exchange; it does not settle those obligations.

This document begins item 6 by co-designing shared controls and the contracts that intelligences inherit. It preserves the seven existing IF identifiers. It supplies one proposed definition per interface in this dossier, not independently varied copies for each consumer. The earlier Contract Model remains explanatory; AOS-00 §5.2 remains the catalogue and allocation baseline. The ownership model was approved by Mark on 4 October; exact contract definitions and normal baseline approval remain pending.

This is not seven completed component specifications. Component requirements, complete record-class schemas, principle/test allocation, operational parameters, threat model, compatibility rules and producer/consumer adoption remain unfinished. Drafting may continue while the AOS-14 research pilot is verified; pilot completion is not a prerequisite for understanding the existing architecture. Findings from the pilot are provisional evidence inputs, not authority to change contracts.

DEC-099 — derived-information restriction rule

Every transformation or combination must preserve the applicable restrictions of its contributing sources. The proposed use of the resulting information must satisfy those restrictions and account for any additional sensitive information the combination reveals. If the system cannot establish the lineage or permitted use sufficiently, it must withhold that use and refer the uncertainty for accountable review.

Applies to summaries, aggregates, embeddings/search indexes, joins, exports and restored derived copies within the declared governed scope. Transformation supplies no wider purpose or permission; conflicting restrictions can prevent the proposed combination. A different treatment requires an evidenced authorised determination under the governing rules, never mere notice or developer discretion. Completed disclosures and unreachable external copies remain explicit limitations with accountable handling; no recall or universal forgetting guarantee is made. AOS-03 REQ-011/012 and the existing necessity, explicit-consent, separate-governance and review-cycle conditions are retained; no new personal-inference exception is created.

AOS-02 owns common lineage/restriction record meaning and the scoped transformation inventory; AOS-03 owns the independent operation/purpose-specific permitted-use assessment. Producers supply attributable source/transformation evidence; receiving components must establish the current permission for their intended use. An unresolved case retains its accountable review duty. Exact profile definitions, evidence sufficiency, source-combination rules, permitted transformations, destination inventory and enforceable withdrawal/recovery behaviour must be fixed and assessed before dependent implementation reliance. This decision adds no default policy, wire schema or automatic override.

DEC-100 — independent minimum control-metadata access

The organisation shall authorise in advance a separate, tightly bounded permission for its checking services to read only the minimum control information necessary to assess a request. That permission must be verifiable independently of whether the requested business action is allowed. It grants no wider access to resident, employee or other operational information. If the required permission or current control information cannot be verified, the affected action remains withheld and the uncertainty is referred for accountable handling.

The control profile identifies the checking service/identity, approving authority and instrument, organisation, permitted metadata records/fields and read operations, assessment purpose, validity/withdrawal/currentness evidence and accountable review route. A service does not obtain this permission from the business request, a model instruction or a favourable result issued by the other check. AOS-01 owns authority for the checking operation; AOS-03 owns permitted metadata use; AOS-02 preserves controlled records; AOS-05 enforces the declared access boundary. Startup and recovery must establish the advance permission/current state through an independently usable approved verification path; no unbounded bootstrap identity, data access or outage privilege is inferred. Separately permitted service/human fallback retains its own authority and duties. Exact profile, verification mechanism and compatibility/evidence are prerequisites before dependent reliance; this direction supplies no credentials, wire schema or live operating permission.

DEC-102 — classification and configuration adequacy

Organisation-defined classifications and settings must demonstrate that they preserve AOS governing constraints before they can be relied upon. Accountable competent assessment is required before use and after material changes; authorisation alone does not establish adequacy. Missing or inadequate settings block the affected reliance and require owned resolution.

Classification considers actual effects on organisational commitments, protected information, rights, services, financial exposure and resource use, including cumulative effects; financial and resource consequences remain distinct. Settings cannot transfer permanently reserved human judgement to automation, remove mandatory reporting, manufacture data-use permission or bypass a prohibition. “Consequential” triggers the applicable controls; it does not automatically require individual human approval for every properly delegated routine action. Organisations retain responsibility for justified local values and legitimate powers. No universal threshold, duration, moral weight or permission is supplied by this decision.

The profile identifies its owner/approver and competent assessor, scope, governing constraints, chosen values/class definitions and reasons, financial/resource units and aggregation assumptions, normal/boundary/cumulative/adverse examples, assessment evidence/limitations, material-change triggers and action on missing or inadequate settings. An AI recommendation or approval record is insufficient by itself. Actual effect and applicable constraints govern assessment, not the label assigned to an operation. A smaller action cannot evade a bound by splitting related work; mandatory reporting and reserved judgement remain protected. AOS-01 owns the governing validation obligation with relevant domain/control owners; AOS-40 assesses scope-specific gate evidence; profile owners supply the actual configuration and review. Dependent effects remain withheld where required, while separately permitted service/reporting duties retain their own powers and owners.

DEC-103 — separate decision, evidence, minutes and execution

AOS shall distinguish the validity and effective time of a human decision, the provenance/authentication and evidential standing of its record, the separate approval status of minutes, and current permission to execute an action. It shall assess decision validity and sufficient evidence under the organisation’s applicable law, legal form, constitution/registered rules, powers and approved evidence/reliance policy. It shall not universally require later minutes approval to bring a decision into existence, nor require a second board approval of a separate decision record where those applicable arrangements do not require it.

Minutes approval and authentication are evidence events, not universal substitutes for the actual decision process. An authenticated record or extract may support reliance through an authorised evidence route, but neither its signature nor its label can manufacture a decision, cure missing powers/conditions or make disputed evidence conclusive. Preserve actual decision, record, authentication/approval, effective and execution times separately; no automatic backdating. Contradictory, missing or insufficient evidence withholds affected automated reliance for accountable human resolution without declaring that an otherwise valid legal decision does not exist. Organisation-specific additional assurance gates may be adopted, but must be labelled as AOS/organisation reliance policy rather than general law. Separately authorised urgent/service duties remain owned. Minute-taker drafting and Company Secretary stewardship remain distinct; human decision powers, protected constitutional processes, boardroom AI exclusion, current authority/data-use checks and DEC-088/089 remain unchanged.

The approved organisation profile binds legal form/jurisdiction, actual constitution/registered rules and powers, valid decision/record/authentication routes, relevant statutory evidence standing, exact required source/validity conditions and competent evidence-assessment/escalation roles. AI cannot infer unseen deliberation or declare legal validity; record the authorised human assessment and limits. No compulsory extra decision-wording approval is invented. A stricter automated-reliance threshold is explicit policy. KX-06 v0.3 is a revised logical view; exact types, code lists, required/conditional fields and reviewed old/new consumer compatibility must be fixed before dependent use. DEC-087 original text is history to the extent superseded; role separation and protected powers remain.

DEC-104 — protected reporting under failure and implicated-person interference

Each mandatory protected-finding category shall have an approved reporting arrangement assessed against both technical failure and interference by implicated people. The profile shall identify the intended recipient, possible conflicts and an appropriately independent authorised alternative; an implicated person shall not be the sole gatekeeper deciding whether the required finding reaches its authorised audience.

Authority/use permissions, minimum report content, recipient verification, custody, route/change dependencies, tracking ownership, acceptance, cover and escalation are established in advance. An external route is included where justified and lawfully authorised; external reporting is not a universal default and a finding creates no extra disclosure power. Planned exercises address failed infrastructure, unavailable/implicated recipients, apparent alternatives controlled by the same implicated gatekeeper and attempted suppression. Missing adequate arrangements remain an explicit owned gap, prevent claims of operational protected-reporting assurance and withhold affected dependent reliance under existing gates; they do not create false delivery, ownership transfer or blanket shutdown of separately authorised service. Original tracking duty persists until actual scoped acceptance.

The organisation’s profile binds each required finding class to normal/alternate authorised audiences, conflict criteria/evidence and accountable assessment, disclosure basis/minimal payload, independently verifiable recipients/permissions, credentials/custody and route/reconfiguration controls, failure/compromise assumptions, tracking/cover/escalation, human fallback, evidence and review triggers. Test both infrastructure/common-dependency independence and organisational release/recipient/custody control; neither implies the other. No administrator, executive sign-off or report-validation step may silently remove a required duty. The declared threat/governance scope and residual limitations must be visible: this is not an unlimited guarantee against every holder of legal/constitutional change powers. Inadequacy returns to an empowered accountable reviewer; the reporting agent cannot invent a recipient or grant itself onward-disclosure authority.

DEC-105 — declared technical control limits and observed assurance

Operational reliance on implemented technical controls requires explicit scope, limits and failure/recovery behaviour supported by accepted observed evidence. The evidence must cover all enabled action paths within the declared control/threat scope, independently of the acting agent; permission-change enforcement points and timing uncertainty; shared cumulative bounds under concurrency; conservative treatment of unknown effects/reservations; and recovery that applies current restrictions without reviving withdrawn authority or repeating completed effects.

The approved control profile fixes exact functions/paths/dependencies/versions, threat assumptions, effect boundary, currentness and revocation/fencing mechanism, timing/uncertainty bounds and units, accounting scopes/consistency and admission, uncertain exposure/release evidence, recovery source/currentness/completeness, permitted fallback/continuing duty, observability/oracle, assessors and objective acceptance conditions. Timeout, cancellation, worker retirement or failover alone cannot prove no effect, release possibly used capacity or permit consequential repetition. No instantaneous global revocation or recall of an irreversible disclosure is promised. Limits are assessed under actual constraints and powers, not silently accepted as waivers of non-waivable safeguards. Missing/failed evidence blocks affected operational-reliance claims with an owned remedy; separately authorised service/reporting duties remain owned and do not acquire extra permissions.

G3 remains authorisation of a bounded build with fixed scope, explicit dependencies/profiles and required separate review; G4 requires implemented observations, and actual operational approval is separate. An explicitly authorised bounded prototype can investigate the mechanisms before operational assurance exists; this decision itself authorises neither a prototype nor live effects. A failed prototype requires investigation of the implementation, assumptions and alternatives; it is not alone proof of architectural impossibility. Model refusal, a generated explanation, intended configuration, an old checkpoint or retrospective log alone is not evidence that all effect paths were enforced or recovery was current. Actual acceptance must link observations to the exact requirement/local-case/variant/profile and endpoint scope, with limitations and competent assessor disposition.

AOS-05 produces control-boundary/dependency observations and declares the implemented limits with authoritative semantic/control owners. AOS-40 evaluates scoped acceptance and preserves build, implementation-verification and operational approval distinctions. Approved profile/instrument owners determine legitimate settings; accountable human assessors disposition limitations without waiving non-waivable constraints.

Unknown, unavailable, not applicable and intentionally withheld are separate meanings. A required but unknown value does not become valid by using a placeholder. Each final contract must specify required/optional/conditional cardinalities and the result when a value is missing. A future encoding must make absence and invalidity machine distinguishable.

Shared delivery and recovery rules proposed for review

  • Separate transport acceptance, semantic acceptance, human ownership and confirmed outcome. A delivery receipt cannot satisfy all four.
  • Validate purpose and authority at the receiving/processing/execution boundary, not only at ingestion. Data admitted for one use may be prohibited for another.
  • Do not assume exactly-once delivery, a global total order or atomic external effect. Each contract defines its own ordering scope and duplicate identity. Unsupported semantics are visible failures.
  • A retry represents another attempt at the same request unless an authorised new request is created. It does not refresh permission, erase prior evidence or establish that the earlier attempt failed.
  • If actual effect is unknown, reconcile it before a potentially consequential repeat. Follow-up, cancellation and compensation each need their own authority; the system cannot promise to undo an external effect.
  • On revocation, context change, consent withdrawal or restart, re-evaluate affected pending work before dependent processing/execution. Define the precise enforcement boundary and race semantics before build; do not imply instantaneous global revocation.
  • Unavailable authority/data-use controls withhold dependent action under DEC-003. A separately authorised escalation or safety fallback may remain possible. Outage does not erase human responsibility.
  • Default constraint explanations identify meaning, authority basis and escalation route. Security/privacy/feasibility exceptions require a recorded reason and review under FAR-R-002; an explanation exception never waives enforcement.

Numerical expiry, retry, acknowledgement, escalation and recovery intervals remain unresolved in §8. They are not implementation defaults.

4.1 AOS-02-IF-001 — Evidence exchange

Semantic owner: proposed AOS-02, with domain source stewardship. Producer → consumer: approved source/domain or human input → authorised domains, GI and governance workflows. Purpose: preserve evidence, provenance, authorised use and correction across component boundaries.

Inputs/outputs: an evidence submission identifies its subject, record class, source/version, observation time, collection purpose, provenance, quality limitations and payload reference. The receiving result distinguishes accepted, accepted-with-restrictions, rejected and unresolved; acceptance records the governed evidence revision and applicable restrictions. A quarantined external input is not an accepted operational fact.

Preconditions/permissions: producer identity and submission right established; consumer processing purpose separately permitted; source-authority status explicit. Observation admission does not establish its truth. A model interpretation cites its input evidence and remains labelled inference.

Lifecycle: submitted → validated/rejected/unresolved → available within permissions → corrected/superseded or retained/deleted under the applicable policy. A correction preserves lineage; its effect on prior analyses and pending actions is assessed rather than retroactively rewriting history. Delivery: acknowledgement means semantic acceptance of a specified revision. Duplicate source event/revision cannot silently overwrite a different payload. Late/out-of-order evidence is labelled and assessed against the relevant subject history, not simply treated as newest truth.

Failure/revocation: invalid scope, unverifiable source or missing required semantics prevents admission for the dependent purpose. Withdrawal of consent affects uses dependent on that basis; it does not silently cancel a separately approved retention duty or authorise continued use. Conflicts go to the named source/data steward through an authorised route. Open: final schemas, authoritative record register, deletion evidence, correction notifications and conflict handling, P02/P04/P05/P08. Draft obligations/cases: SC-R01/R02/R03; SC-V01/V02/V06.

4.2 AOS-01-IF-001 — Authority determination

Semantic owner: proposed AOS-01. Producer → consumer: authorised control function, evaluating requester/context → executor and relevant routing/exchange controls. Purpose: decide whether a precisely identified requested action is currently permitted within approved instruments.

Inputs: request identity/revision; actor and human principal where applicable; action type/target and material parameters; domain/context/conditions; organisation scope; purpose; relevant data-use determination; policy/delegation versions; required human decision evidence. Output: allow, deny or unresolved, with action binding, checked versions, conditions, reason, validity and determination evidence. Control unavailability is a distinct operational result, not allow. An allow with conditions requires every applicable condition to be satisfied; it is not a fourth permissive category that avoids checking.

Preconditions: organisation-approved consequential-action classification and validated schedule configuration; no core waiver; legitimate data use established where needed. Technical credentials and GI recommendations cannot substitute for delegation.

Lifecycle: requested → evaluated → determined → consumed/expired/invalidated. Consumption is evidence of use, not proof of effect. Before execution, the executor checks the relevant validity/context/revocation boundary. Exact freshness, atomicity and change races remain P03; no indefinite cache licence exists.

Failure/revocation: deny, unresolved, unavailable, expired, revoked or mismatched action binding withholds dependent execution. A changed target/amount/purpose is not covered by a determination for the original request. Refer uncertainty through a separately authorised internal route. Replays/retries do not broaden the grant. Open: actor assurance P04, race/validity P03, classification/configuration P07, failure fallback P06/P09, explanation exceptions P10. Draft obligations/cases: SC-R04/R05/R06/R15; SC-V03/V04/V09/V10.

4.3 AOS-13-IF-001 — Escalation lifecycle

Semantic owner: proposed AOS-13; human decision/ownership records use AOS-30. Producer → consumer: authorised detecting domain or GI → routing/workflow service, accountable human role and oversight. Purpose: get a finding to the authorised audience and keep responsibility visible until disposition and required follow-up.

Inputs: case/trigger identity, evidence, classification/uncertainty, internal routing delegation, required audience, responsible role, receipt/response windows and cover policy. Outputs: routing result, delivery evidence, explicit human acknowledgement/ownership, deadline events, decision/disposition and linked outcome/follow-up.

Proposed lifecycle: created → routed → delivered → human-owned → disposition recorded → follow-up pending → closed. Unacknowledged, overdue, recipient unavailable and routing failed are explicit exceptions. Delivery alone does not transfer accountable ownership; the sending workflow retains tracking responsibility until the approved ownership-transfer condition is evidenced. Exact institutional allocation remains P06 rather than being assigned to an unnamed person by software.

Closure: identify who may close, the disposition, remaining obligations, evidence and any authorised termination with an unresolved result. Closed must not mean repaired, safe or successful unless the applicable outcome evidence supports that meaning. A linked action can remain open after an initial decision.

Failure/retries: correlate repeated signals without suppressing materially changed evidence. Expired deadlines activate approved cover/escalation rules, not guessed recipients. Mandatory-audience findings cannot be silently removed by an operational suppression instruction; protected routing and assurance require definition. Internal escalation is distinct from external contact and each requires its own delegation. Open: P06/P09, duplicate/materiality rules P02, protected audience mechanism P12. Draft obligations/cases: SC-R07/R08/R09; SC-V05/V08/V11.

4.4 AOS-20-IF-001 — Canonical analysis

Semantic owner: proposed AOS-20; AOS-24 owns rendering fidelity, not an independent conclusion. Producer → consumer: AOS-21–23/25 under the GI charter → AOS-24, authorised executives/humans and AOS-30. Purpose: preserve one identified analysis revision, its evidence and review status across presentations.

Inputs/outputs: analysis identity/revision, purpose/telos and governance-source references, state/evidence snapshot, assumptions, options including the infeasible/unacceptable option when material, consequences/trade-offs, uncertainty, excluded coverage, dissent/conflict and requested human question. Outputs include canonical analysis plus review/disclosure status. Model/provider and evaluation references identify relevant limitations without requiring private model reasoning.

Proposed lifecycle: draft → submitted for the specified review → reviewed/returned → superseded or withdrawn. Review status identifies reviewer, scope and exact revision; reviewed is not approved action. Missing executive validation must remain visible where the approved workflow requires it. Model-generated confidence is not validated calibration.

Delivery/failure: renderings identify the same canonical revision and review state. Audience-specific redaction/translation preserves material substance and limitations within authorised disclosure; a translation cannot silently become a new canonical conclusion. Evidence correction or policy change flags affected analyses for reassessment under P02/P11; an old result is not automatically current. Conflict between incommensurable values goes to Mode 2, with unresolved judgement explicit. Open: GI charter roles, review status and evaluation P07/P11; disclosure P05; stale analysis P02. Draft obligations/cases: SC-R10/R11; SC-V02/V07/V11.

4.5 AOS-30-IF-001 — Human decision/debrief

Semantic owner: proposed AOS-30; approved corpus admission belongs to AOS-21 within its custodian's delegation. Producer → consumer: authorised human via permitted post-meeting process → AOS-02/20/21/22/23/24/25 where purpose-permitted and relied on, and authorised executors through separate action controls. Purpose: make a human decision and its consequences reconstructable without observing deliberation.

Inputs/outputs: human-provided post-meeting account; actual decision-making body/role and acting persons; decision and scope; evidence/analysis references; approved rationale, rejected options and reasons; conditions, dissent/limitations where authorised; effective time and follow-up owner/date.
