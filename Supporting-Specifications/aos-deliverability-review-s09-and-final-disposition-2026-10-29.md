## S-09 disposition

Mark agreed to close question 9 without architectural amendment. The review did not demonstrate a contradiction between AOS-12 REQ-020 (prohibited individual emotion/health/private-conduct inference from the specified signals) and REQ-029 (permitted minimal-evidence workforce safety referral through an empowered human route, without diagnosis or invented emergency powers). A reported illness or workplace hazard can be referred without generating a prohibited personal inference.

Operational task/ownership records do not automatically become permissible evidence for personnel surveillance. Existing purpose, authority and data-use rules apply; notice alone supplies no processing permission. AOS-12 REQ-026 and its surrounding purpose/sensitive/cross-domain controls retain their meanings. No new inference exception, surveillance permission, workflow requirement or committee power is introduced. Any specific residual ambiguity should be addressed as a targeted explanatory/editorial correction, with its actual evidence identified; no such ambiguity is silently assumed resolved by this disposition.
