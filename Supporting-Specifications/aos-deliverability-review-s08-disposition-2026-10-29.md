## Disposition

S-08: closed as rejected/reclassified to existing governance procedure. The earlier review conflated a possible oversight method with a missing architectural obligation. It does not establish that the committee lacks the responsibility or that new GI requirements are needed. Candidate evaluation methods may be considered through the committee’s existing remit and legitimate powers; they are not mandated by this review.

No new DEC number, technical requirement, test, profile rule, committee power, permission or architecture version is introduced. DEC-099–105 remain as previously approved; no other question is reopened or resolved by this disposition. S-09 remains for final triage. No running-system work or test execution occurred.
