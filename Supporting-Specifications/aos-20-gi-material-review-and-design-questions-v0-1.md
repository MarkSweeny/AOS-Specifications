## 1. Review conclusion

The existing material supplies a substantial foundation for the GI charter: cross-domain synthesis, a governed corpus, interpretation and option preparation, faithful presentation, and an authorising/obligating charter. RP v6 already incorporates the working paper's precedent-drift and protected-escalation principles. These are governing constraints, not fresh optional proposals.

The current AOS-00/Map v0.18 and kernel v0.6 demarcation governs reconciliation: GI interprets and prepares; humans adjudicate Mode 2; AOS-14 handles external exchange; shared controls enforce permissions. AOS-20 defines the responsibilities of GI as a whole, while 21–25 and 30 supply detailed component workflows. This review does not reopen approved kernel decisions.

Seven choices were submitted at charter/workflow level and are now approved under DEC-079–085 (see §6). Organisational appointments, particular recipient identities, numerical thresholds, review deadlines, implementation services and model evaluation evidence remain later commissioning dependencies. The questions below concern reusable architecture, not a single repair process or a single-organisation structure.

## 3. Corrections required without reopening settled principles

- Replace the older GIL description of GI as a sixth peer domain with GI as the whole AOS-20–25 layer above the five sensing domains.
- Translate adjudication/conscience/membrane language into specified software duties and accountable human judgement. GI cannot confer authority, adjudicate Mode 2 or govern by changing the kernel's controls.
- Preserve synthesis/interpretation versus rendering, and charter versus executable function. AOS-23's artefact assessment is an explicit capability within GI; no additional peer intelligence is introduced.
- Preserve permanent human Mode 2, boardroom exclusion and authoritative human-approved post-meeting records. Conversational confirmation cannot be an indirect meeting-capture channel.
- Preserve source hierarchy, current authority/use, protected escalation, acknowledged handoff, human case closure and conservative recovery rules. No protected finding is held indefinitely for a discretionary management sign-off.
- Reconcile the GIL's blanket review-before-UDL wording with its own urgent-alert UDL publication and the canonical-analysis review-state contract. Separate storage of provisional evidence, authorised challenge access and formal reliance; Q06 resolves the remaining workflow choice.
- Retain CAP-01's shared evidence base and its no-ranking/no-recommendation constraint between persona readings. Personas cannot create separate competing factual realities.
- Do not adopt historic numerical confidence thresholds, particular products, 2015 regulatory references or claims of solved technical capability as present validated defaults. Current regulatory or standard-specific obligations need primary-source verification when used in the specifications.

## 4. Submitted design questions and recommendations — approved disposition in §6

### Q01 — charter amendment powers

**Question:** should GI's core permissions, prohibitions and mandatory duties be a protected board-approved charter, with operational profiles maintained separately?

**Recommendation:** yes. The board approves and amends the charter's constitutional core; technical administrators and ordinary delegates cannot weaken it. Organisation-specific routes, recipients, timing and analytical settings sit in separately approved operational profiles under AOS-01 change powers. A profile change cannot dilute a core duty or broaden authority. Applicable organisational constitutional instruments determine the precise process; do not universally prescribe a special resolution. This resolves the working paper's “same bar as telos” proposal without treating every parameter change as a telos amendment.

### Q02 — corpus steward's delegated powers

**Question:** what may the human corpus steward approve, and what must go to another empowered authority?

**Recommendation:** establish a human Governance Knowledge Steward function, often located with the company secretary/governance team. Delegate routine source admission, provenance, version/currency maintenance and faithful capture of already approved decisions within defined policy. Separate verification of source status from organisational adoption of a new obligation. Disputed authority, materially changed meaning, new normative interpretations and conflicts go to the appropriate authorised decision-maker; the steward cannot invent constitutional authority. Oversight needs an independent reading of the evidence, including independent-of-GI preparation of the annual report. Role names and organisational location may vary; powers and separation remain explicit.

### Q03 — confirmation and precedent

**Question:** when does a human confirmation become an authoritative interpretation or precedent?

**Recommendation:** distinguish conversational understanding, reviewed analysis, an empowered human decision and approved precedent. “Yes, that is what I meant” in a Petra exchange does not itself amend the corpus. Approval binds exact wording/revision, decision-maker's powers, rationale, context, limitations and applicability; ordinary interpretations may be approved within an explicit human delegation, while constitutional disputes/amendments follow protected routes. GI can propose and challenge precedent but cannot approve its own interpretation. Preserve differing rulings and review their cumulative drift against the founding statement; do not treat the latest ruling as universal truth.

### Q04 — proactive GI review

**Question:** may GI initiate cross-domain review without waiting for a human request?

**Recommendation:** yes, within explicitly approved purposes, sources and event/cycle triggers. GI should notice inconsistency, emerging patterns, stale governance material and purpose/precedent drift and initiate internal review. No additional sensing or private resident/worker access follows from this authority. Automated noticing and reassessment do not authorise external contact, commitments, operational effects or Mode 2 resolution. Persistent/combined patterns are assessed alongside individual events so that thresholds do not hide accumulation.

### Q05 — required escalation classes and routing

**Question:** which findings must be protected from suppression or discretionary delay?

**Recommendation:** require protected internal escalation for credible material indications of: telos/constitutional conflict or drift; Mode 2 encroachment/authority bypass; attempted suppression or control tampering; failure to meet a required reporting/compliance obligation; material safety/welfare implications; and evidence/assurance failure that makes an important governance conclusion unsafe to rely on. These are indications for human assessment, not AI verdicts of wrongdoing. Organisation-approved criteria add context, urgency, accountable recipients/cover and response times; no invented universal thresholds. If the normal recipient is implicated or fails to respond, route through a separately authorised alternative to the appropriate independent governance authority. Apply approved permission, minimal payload, acknowledgement/escalation and failed-route rules from the kernel. Protected reporting remains owned even when normal operation is stopped.

### Q06 — review before publication or reliance

**Question:** must every GI output await human review before anyone can see it, or should review depend on the intended use?

**Recommendation:** use explicit graduated review states. Permitted provisional analyses can be stored, interrogated and used to alert authorised reviewers, visibly labelled with uncertainty, source coverage and review limits. Formal governance assessments and material decision-support conclusions need the designated human validation before being represented as reviewed or placed in the applicable formal decision workflow. Action authority remains separate in every state. Urgent protected escalation does not wait for the executive review it may challenge. A raw model confidence score cannot substitute for source assurance or human review. Preserve one identified canonical analysis revision and review status across permitted presentations.

### Q07 — independent challenge access

**Question:** should GI's challenge output always be shared with management immediately, or may NEDs/the relevant independent governance body have a protected preparation window?

**Recommendation:** allow an explicitly governed independent preparation window for authorised NED/committee challenge work, including CAP-01 divergence analysis. The executive whose paper is being tested cannot tune or suppress that challenge profile. This is a timing/access rule over the same canonical evidence and analysis, not a second secret factual corpus. Relevant management receives the permitted findings/questions in time to correct facts and respond, under an approved workflow. The window cannot delay protected mandatory reporting or conceal material governance evidence from its required audience; no AI enters live deliberation. Board-approved scope, permitted recipients and exceptions belong in AOS-03/24/30 and the CAP-01 profile. Do not adopt an always-secret or always-shared default for every GI use.

Research observes, analyses, challenges and proposes; governed human decisions and controlled change determine adaptation. Relevant findings must identify primary sources/date/coverage, current GI baseline and affected element, evidence and uncertainty, implications/options and cross-component impacts. Accepted/rejected/deferred/unresolved dispositions retain accountable owners; urgent protected findings use the approved routes. A single agent or multiple processes are implementation choices, not numerical defaults.
